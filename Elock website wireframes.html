Mobile rules

  • Always-visible contact. Sticky bottom bar with Call, WhatsApp and Quote. Most installers will contact you from site, with a phone.
  • Tap targets at least 44 px high, with space between them.
  • Accordions for long content (specs, FAQs, the three-beat story) so pages stay short.
  • One column. The door-system diagram becomes a vertical list; featured products swipe sideways.
  • No wide tables. Spec tables become two-column key and value lists.
  • Forms: correct keyboard per field (email, phone), no country dropdown on the home form.
  • Images: serve WebP/AVIF in responsive sizes, lazy-load everything below the first screen.
  • Menu: a full-screen overlay with Products first, the quote button pinned at the bottom.

Check before launch

  • Run PageSpeed Insights on mobile. Target Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 s.
  • Test on one low-end Android phone, one iPhone, and a tablet.
  • Test forms, tap-to-call and WhatsApp links on real devices.
