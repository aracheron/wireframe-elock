## 2026-10-30 - aria-label on generic elements\n**Learning:** Screen readers may ignore `aria-label` on generic `div` elements without a semantic role. Adding a semantic role (e.g., `role="group"` or `role="region"`) is required for the `aria-label` to be properly announced.\n**Action:** When adding `aria-label` to a generic container, ensure it also has an appropriate semantic role.
